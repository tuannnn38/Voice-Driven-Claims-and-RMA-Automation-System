Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the Voice Agent ask for my phone number on a web call?

Unlike traditional phone calls (PSTN) that automatically pass your caller ID (ANI), browser-based WebRTC calls are anonymous. Reading or entering your registered phone number alongside your Order Number and Postal Code ensures secure account matching.

Can I update or cancel an existing claim during the call?

Yes. Once verified, you can create a new claim, check real-time RMA status, update defect details, or cancel an open claim by voice or through the xApp companion interface.
